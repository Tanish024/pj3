# Sample Carousel Exports

This directory contains the exported assets for the sample carousel:
**"5 Things to Check Before Choosing a Vet for Your Pet"**

### Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 Portrait (Instagram standard, 1080 × 1350 px)
- **Design Aesthetic**: Warm Editorial
- **Typography**: Fraunces Serif (Headlines & Numerals) + Inter Sans (Body Copy)
- **Palette**: Sand (`#F6F1E7`), Ink (`#1C1B19`), Terracotta (`#D9633B`)
- **Margins**: 96px uniform inset
- **Word Count**: All slides < 25 words

### Files
- `slide-01.png`: Slide 01 — Hook Cover (Ink theme with terracotta underline accent)
- `slide-02.png`: Slide 02 — Problem Statement (Sand theme, proximity vs quality)
- `slide-03.png`: Slide 03 — Tip 1: Emergency availability (Ink theme, 24/7 care)
- `slide-04.png`: Slide 04 — Tip 2: Qualifications & depth (Sand theme, surgeon credentials)
- `slide-05.png`: Slide 05 — Tip 3: Clean, safe facility (Ink theme, separate recovery areas)
- `slide-06.png`: Slide 06 — Tip 4: Transparent pricing (Sand theme, itemized estimates)
- `slide-07.png`: Slide 07 — Tip 5: Handling & temperament (Sand theme, Fear-Free handling)
- `slide-08.png`: Slide 08 — Action CTA (Ink theme, brand handle, action pill)
- `sample-carousel.pdf`: 8-page compiled document carousel ready for LinkedIn PDF uploads
